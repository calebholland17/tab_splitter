How to set up a tab

1. Scan the receipt, then check the items

The scanner is usually right, but always scroll through the item list afterward and compare it against the receipt — names, quantities, and per-item prices.

Known quirk: for a line like "4 Miller Lite  $24.00", the scanner sometimes keeps $24.00 as the price for each of the 4, instead of $6.00 each. Always check that price × qty roughly matches the line on the receipt.

2. Fixing a price with the ÷ split toggle

If an item's price is really the total for everyone sharing it, click the × button next to it to flip it to ÷. The number you type is now the total — it's divided by the quantity automatically, and you'll see "= $X.XX each" appear.

3. Quick math in any price field

You can type a calculation directly into any item or fee price field — e.g. type 24/4 and tab or click away, and it resolves to 6.00. Handy for fixing a scanner mistake without doing the division yourself.

4. Double-check the total

If you entered a receipt total during scanning, a warning appears if your item/fee totals don't match it — that's usually a sign something above still needs fixing.
