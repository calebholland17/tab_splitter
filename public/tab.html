How this works

Pick your name

Tap your name once at the start. It's remembered on this device, so you won't have to pick it again unless you tap Change.

Claim your items

Tap an item to claim it; tap again to release it. Items someone else already claimed are greyed out.

What you owe

Your total includes your share of tax, surcharge, and gratuity, split proportionally based on what you claimed — not divided evenly.

Paying

Tap Pay on Venmo — it opens Venmo with the amount and note pre-filled. The host will mark you as paid once they see the payment come through.
